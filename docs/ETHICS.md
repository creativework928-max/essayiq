# Ethics

Automated essay scoring can affect educational opportunities and student agency. EssayIQ therefore separates its learned holistic score from derived writing indicators, documents uncertainty limitations, avoids sensitive-attribute inference, and discourages high-stakes autonomous decisions.

Potential risks include annotation bias, linguistic variation, benchmark shift, gaming of measurable signals, false confidence, privacy exposure, and overreliance on a single number. Human review remains necessary for consequential decisions.
